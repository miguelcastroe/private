Profile

I work from problems that do not quite add up: things others have walked past, openings that are not obvious until you stay with them.

My work as a creative leader is to make it easier for good ideas to show up, and harder for them to get lost. Raising the bar. Protecting the craft. Organizing teams around something worth building.

Way of Thinking

Reading between the lines of a brief. Cutting to what matters. Keeping standards under pressure.

The work is not only making things look intelligent. It is making something previously unclear easier to understand, use or feel.
